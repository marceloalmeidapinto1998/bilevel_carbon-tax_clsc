\subsection{Extended tax-range analysis}
\label{app:tax_extended}

\Cref{sec:exp:tradeoff} reports that two base instances place the optimal tax
at the base ceiling (200 USD/t) and that the emission--tax response saturates.
\Cref{tab:app_tax_extended} substantiates this by re-solving the four screened
base instances with the leader ceiling lifted to $400$ and $800$ USD/t. Lifting the
ceiling moves the lexicographic optimum to a much higher tax rate on three of
the four instances, yet the corresponding emissions barely change: the largest
abatement unlocked by quadrupling the admissible tax is $0.07\%$. For
\texttt{STD\_I17\_M8\_T3} the emissions-minimising rate is not even
point-identified --- it shifts from $140$ to $310$ USD/t between the two extended
ranges while emissions stay flat to within solver tolerance.

\subsection{Solver coverage and external benchmarks}
\label{app:coverage}

The numerical study rests on 95 validated runs: the eight base instances and
twelve design-lever scenarios are each solved with the lexicographic exact
workflow \texttt{lf\_lex\_exact} and, on the base instances, cross-checked
against the independent enumeration \texttt{enum\_lf\_lex\_exact} (identical
leader decisions); the remaining 75 runs form the sensitivity suite. No
result directory or solver entry point implements a KKT single-level
reformulation or an exhaustive-grid bilevel benchmark beyond this enumeration
cross-check, so we make no cross-method performance comparison.
