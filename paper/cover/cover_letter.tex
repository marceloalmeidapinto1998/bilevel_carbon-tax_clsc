\opening{Dear Editor,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled
\textbf{``Optimal Carbon Pricing of Closed-Loop Supply Chains:
A Global-Optimisation of a Bi-Level Mixed-Integer Problem''} for consideration for publication in
\textit{Journal Name}.

\textbf{Scope and novelty.}
This paper addresses the strategic interaction between a government
setting a carbon tax rate and a supply chain operator designing a
closed-loop network in response.
We propose a bilevel mixed-integer programming model that explicitly
captures this Stackelberg leader--follower structure.
To our knowledge, this is the first work to combine a bilevel
carbon taxation framework with a closed-loop supply chain network
design problem including integer facility decisions.

\textbf{Methodological contribution.}
We develop a Deterministic Bounding Procedure (DBP) with proven
convergence guarantees and provide reproducible computational
artifacts for the implemented DBP workflows.
