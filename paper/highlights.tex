\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item A regulator sets a carbon tax anticipating how a firm redesigns its supply chain
\item The optimal tax lies at a switching price, where the firm moves to a cleaner network
\item Enumerating switching prices is fastest when they are few, bounding when many
\item Bounding still certifies the tax when the firm's own problem cannot be solved
\item A fixed tax at the European carbon price overpays or falls short of the optimal tax
\end{itemize}
