\section{Mathematical Model}
\label{sec:model}

We formulate the \CLSC design problem with the carbon tax as a leader
decision variable, as a bilevel mixed-integer programme (\BLMIP). A regulator (leader) selects a
carbon-tax index; a supply chain operator (follower) then optimises
network design and operations in response. \Cref{sec:model:assumptions}
states the modelling assumptions. \Cref{sec:model:notation} establishes
notation and auxiliary expressions. \Cref{sec:model:bilevel} presents the
compact bilevel programme and a complexity result.
\Cref{sec:model:leader,sec:model:follower} develop the regulator's and
operator's problems in detail.
