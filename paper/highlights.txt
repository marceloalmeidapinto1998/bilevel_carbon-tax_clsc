Highlights

- A regulator sets a carbon tax anticipating how a firm redesigns its supply chain
- The optimal tax lies at a switching price, where the firm moves to a cleaner network
- Enumerating switching prices is fastest when they are few, bounding when many
- Bounding still certifies the tax when the firm's own problem cannot be solved
- A fixed tax at the European carbon price overpays or falls short of the optimal tax
