Highlights

- A regulator sets a carbon tax anticipating how an operator redesigns its supply chain
- An optimal tax lies at the lowest tax allowed or at a switching price of the operator
- Enumerating the switching prices traces how emissions respond to the whole tax range
- Bounding still brackets the optimum when the operator's problem is not solved exactly
- A tax fixed at the European allowance price overpays or abates less than the optimum
